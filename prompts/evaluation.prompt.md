# Task

Evaluate the subject identified by the context using the criteria above.

Follow any scoring scale, weighting, output sections, recommendation labels, and
special interpretation rules specified in the context. Those instructions take
precedence over a generic format.

For every scored dimension or conclusion:

- cite supporting or contradicting transcript evidence with timestamps;
- name the speaker when attribution matters;
- distinguish what the subject raised spontaneously from what emerged only
  after another participant or facilitator prompted it;
- state when evidence is missing or ambiguous rather than filling the gap;
- treat speaker labels as user-assigned and potentially inaccurate, and lower
  confidence when the conclusion depends on attribution.

End with the final assessment requested by the context and a short section named
`Where this evaluation might be wrong` covering doubtful transcription,
speaker-label accuracy, speaker attribution, thin evidence, and interpretation
uncertainty. State whether any material conclusion would change if a disputed
label belonged to another participant.
